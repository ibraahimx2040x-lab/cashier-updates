# food_v1.tflite

Google AIY "Vision classifier: food V1" (MobileNet V1, ~2,000 dishes),
from TensorFlow Hub / Kaggle Models, licensed under the Apache License 2.0
(https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0). Used unmodified, on the
device only, to suggest what a meal photo shows; nutrition values come from
the app's own food list and the user confirms every entry.
