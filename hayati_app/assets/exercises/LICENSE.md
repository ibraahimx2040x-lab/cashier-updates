# Exercise photos

The start and end photos in this folder come from
[free-exercise-db](https://github.com/yuhonas/free-exercise-db), released into
the public domain under [the Unlicense](https://unlicense.org). They were
cropped to 4:3 and resized to 480×360.

Each photo was checked by eye against the exercise's own steps in
`lib/domain/fitness/exercises.dart`; exercises without a photo that shows the
same movement have none, rather than a near match.
